Weekly rhythm

A realistic study schedule

If you do both tracks at the same time, keep the weekday sessions short and make the weekend project non-negotiable.

Mon Video lesson + edit drill

45 minutes learning, 60 minutes cutting footage.

Tue Animation lesson + motion drill

45 minutes learning, 60 minutes animating a simple scene.

Wed Video technique day

Practice audio, color, text, effects, or pacing.

Thu Animation technique day

Practice timing, spacing, posing, camera, or rigging.

Fri Review and export

Watch your work, write 3 fixes, export one version.

Sat/Sun Weekend project

Finish one small project. Do not keep it forever as a draft.

Minimum plan: 1 hour per day

  • 20 minutes tutorial or reading
  • 35 minutes hands-on practice
  • 5 minutes notes: what worked, what failed, next fix

Stronger plan: 2 hours per day

  • 30 minutes learning
  • 75 minutes project work
  • 15 minutes review, export, and backup

Final target

What you should have after 3 months

Video editing portfolio

  • One 30 to 60 second social video
  • One 2 minute story, vlog, or mini documentary
  • One polished edit showing audio, color, titles, and pacing
  • Three before/after breakdown clips

Photoshop/design portfolio

  • Three YouTube thumbnails with different styles
  • One poster or event flyer
  • One photo retouching before/after
  • One social media carousel or ad set

Animation portfolio

  • One bouncing ball study with notes
  • One walk cycle or looping character motion
  • One 10 second acting, expression, or camera scene
  • One 20 to 30 second finished animated short

How to review your work

  • Is the first 3 seconds clear?
  • Can someone understand the story without your explanation?
  • Are sound levels comfortable?
  • Does every shot or frame have a reason to exist?

Career paths

Job opportunities in the UK and Egypt

The best early goal is not one dream title. Build a small portfolio that proves you can edit video, design strong images, and animate simple motion. Then apply to junior roles, apprenticeships, internships, assistant roles, and freelance projects.
